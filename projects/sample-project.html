Trade Flow Analysis

This project uses ArcGIS Pro to analyze bilateral trade patterns across Sub-Saharan Africa. By mapping trade flows, tariff structures, and transportation corridors, the analysis reveals key patterns in regional economic integration and identifies opportunities for policy intervention to strengthen intra-African trade.

Key Findings

  • Intra-regional trade accounts for only 15% of total Sub-Saharan African trade, significantly below other regions.
  • Transportation corridor gaps between landlocked countries create measurable friction in bilateral trade volumes.
  • Tariff reduction under AfCFTA could increase intra-African trade by an estimated 33% by 2030.
