GIS Spatial Analysis

Census & Building Spatial Analysis

A comparative spatial analysis examining census tract demographics and building infrastructure between current (2020) conditions and projected 2100 scenarios. The project joins census data with building footprints to assess how demographic shifts and climate projections may impact urban infrastructure and population distribution.

Project Screenshot

Add screenshot: assets/images/projects/spatial-analysis.png

Key Aspects

  • Joined census demographic data with building footprint shapefiles for both 2020 and 2100 projections.
  • Computed building-level statistics to quantify infrastructure exposure under future scenarios.
  • Visualized spatial patterns of demographic change and building vulnerability across census tracts.
